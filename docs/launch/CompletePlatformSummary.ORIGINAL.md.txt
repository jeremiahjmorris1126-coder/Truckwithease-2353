## What TruckWithEase Is

TruckWithEase is an all-in-one fleet intelligence platform built for trucking companies, owner-operators, and fleet managers of all sizes — from a single-truck owner-op to a 50-truck fleet. It replaces 15–20 separate apps and subscriptions with a single command center that handles dispatch, compliance, maintenance, driver management, load sourcing, financial reporting, and AI-powered decision-making.

The platform is a progressive web app (PWA) — meaning it runs in a web browser but can be installed on any phone or tablet like a native app, with no App Store required. It also has a Google Play submission guide built-in for those who want the full app-store experience.

All data is stored securely in the platform's own backend, never in a third party's hands.

## MAINTENANCE & DIAGNOSTICS

### MaintEase (`/maintenance`)
The full fleet maintenance intelligence system.
- **Photo Scan**: Take a photo of any engine component — AI identifies the part and potential issues
- **DTC Code Reader**: Enter any fault code — full library of SPN/J1939 and OBD codes decoded
- **Quantum Predictive Engine**: AI predicts failures before they happen based on mileage, hours, and patterns
- **Service Log**: Full history of every repair, every part, every cost — saved permanently
- **Asset Health Index**: Visual health score for every vehicle in the fleet
- **Brand Studio**: Custom fleet branding (colors, logo, white-label) unlocks at 10+ assets
- **16 Customizable Modules**: Fleet managers toggle exactly the modules they need
- All records saved to the fleet's permanent maintenance archive

### INDEX=MECHANIC (`/mechanic`)
Legend-level AI diagnostic agent — the best truck mechanic in the world, available 24/7.

**4 Modes:**

**1. Diagnose a Problem**
- Describe any symptom in plain language (or paste a fault code)
- THE MECHANIC decodes it using a professional knowledge base covering: engine, fuel system, air system, aftertreatment (DEF/DPF), transmission, brakes, tires, electrical, cooling, steering, clutch, and fuel economy
- Brand-specific responses: select Volvo, Peterbilt, Kenworth, Freightliner, International, Mack, or Western Star and get known issues + service bulletins for that specific brand
- Multi-keyword matching catches every variation of how a driver might describe a problem
- One-tap "Log to MaintEase" saves the full diagnosis to the permanent service record

**2. ELD Fault Scan**
- Select your tracking device (Geotab, Motive/KeepTruckin, Samsara, Azuga, or manual entry)
- Paste in any SPN or DTC codes from the screen
- THE MECHANIC instantly decodes: what it means, what caused it, and the exact fix procedure
- 19 codes in the built-in library with full root-cause and repair guidance
- One-tap escalation to full chat diagnosis
- Saved to permanent service records with one tap

**3. DVIR Inspection (with Memory)**
- Full DOT-compliant pre-trip and post-trip checklists (60+ inspection points across 8 categories)
- **Memory**: The agent automatically loads the prior day's DVIR — any defect not seen before is flagged in orange as "NEW DAMAGE"
- **Photo capture**: Tap the camera icon on any failed item — photo is taken with the phone camera and attached to that specific defect
- **Damage notes**: Free-text notes field opens on every defect for precise description
- **Auto-alert to insurance**: Enter your insurance company name — any new damage detected automatically flags the report for your insurer with all photos attached
- **Auto-wire to MaintEase**: Any defects found create a work order in MaintEase automatically — the fleet manager sees it immediately

**4. PM Planner**
- Enter current odometer and engine hours
- THE MECHANIC calculates your complete preventive maintenance schedule across 20 service intervals
- Color-coded: overdue (red), due soon (orange), on track (green)
- Covers: oil changes, fuel filters, air filters, coolant SCA, belts, transmission fluid, differentials, wheel bearings, DPF cleaning, DOT annual inspection, and 10 more

## WHAT MAKES TRUCKWITHEASE DIFFERENT

1. **Everything in one place.** No switching between 12 apps — dispatch, compliance, maintenance, GPS, comms, training, finance are all one platform with one login.

2. **AI that actually knows trucking.** The agents understand CDL law, FMCSA regulations, DOT inspection standards, J1939 fault codes, and brand-specific truck issues. This isn't a generic AI — it's been trained on trucking.

3. **THE MECHANIC costs nothing extra.** A single diagnostic call at a shop is $150-200. THE MECHANIC is available 24/7, decodes every code, and guides drivers through the fix before they ever call a shop.

4. **DVIR with memory and insurance.** No other platform compares today's inspection to yesterday's and automatically flags new damage — then attaches the photos and notifies insurance. This alone is worth the platform.

5. **Built for owner-ops and fleets equally.** A single owner-operator gets everything they need. A 50-truck fleet manager gets fleet-wide visibility, per-driver intelligence, and multi-unit asset management.

6. **No hardware required.** GPS tracking from the driver's phone. ELD codes entered from the dashboard display. Photo diagnosis from the phone camera. Zero upfront hardware cost.

7. **Grows with you.** Brand Studio unlocks at 10+ assets. Fleet features scale automatically.
